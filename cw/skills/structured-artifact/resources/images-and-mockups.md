# Images and Mockups

Load when embedding, annotating, or comparing images and mockups.

Label proposals and illustrations so they cannot be mistaken for observed
results. Distinguish illustrative dimensions from measurements.

Crop to the relevant detail while preserving enough context to understand it.
Keep scale and alignment consistent when comparing images.

Keep annotations anchored to the image as it resizes. SVG overlays can share
its coordinate system; separately positioned HTML labels need equivalent
scaling. Check that callouts do not cover the detail they explain.

Use HTML/CSS when a mockup needs selectable text or meaningful interaction;
use an image when captured appearance is the evidence. Mark nonfunctional
mockup controls as illustrative.

Provide alt text for informative images and an adjacent explanation for complex
ones. Check readability at the displayed size.

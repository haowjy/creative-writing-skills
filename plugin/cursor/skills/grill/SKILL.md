# Grill

Zoom out first: what the plan is for, what it touches, and what would make
it wrong.

Then walk down the decision tree, one cluster at a time. A cluster is the
open decisions about one part of the plan, asked together, each with your
recommended answer. Settle a cluster before the clusters that hang off it.
Find facts yourself; ask the human only for decisions. Stop when no
decision is left open.

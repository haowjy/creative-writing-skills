# Story Planning

Use this for pre-page decisions: what the story wants, what options exist, and how chosen direction becomes outline structure. Do not use it for production prose or critique.

Load only the resource needed:

- `resources/creative-direction.md` — author-facing direction and option synthesis.
- `resources/brainstorming.md` — exploratory capture, source tagging, and the brainstorm report. Deep guides by topic: `resources/brainstorming/chapter-planning.md`, `resources/brainstorming/character-development.md`, `resources/brainstorming/continuity-timeline.md`, `resources/brainstorming/worldbuilding.md`.
- `resources/story-architecture.md` — arc, chapter, scene, and beat structure. Deep guides: `resources/story-architecture/arc-design.md` (macro arcs), `resources/story-architecture/chapter-and-scene.md` (writing an outline: what each beat carries), `resources/story-architecture/structural-problems.md` (diagnosing plot/pacing issues).
